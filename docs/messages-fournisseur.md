# Messages pour le fournisseur (TL-0191)

À envoyer en anglais. Retirer les questions déjà réglées.

## Message 1 : conformité, prix, délais

```
Hello,

My online shop in France is ready to launch with your lamp TL-0191. I will choose my supplier this week, based on complete answers. Please reply within 48 hours.

1. Certificates: CE, RoHS, and battery documents (UN38.3 / MSDS). Without CE I cannot sell in Europe.
2. Price per unit for 1 / 30 / 50 / 100 pcs, shipping to France included.
3. Delivery time to France with tracking. Do you have an EU warehouse?
4. Do you offer dropshipping (ship 1 unit directly to each customer in France)?
5. Sample: price and shipping time.

Thank you,
[Your name]
```

## Message 2 : produit et colis

```
More details I need for my product page:
1. Battery capacity (mAh), battery life (max / min brightness), charging time, charging port (USB-C?).
2. Color temperatures of the 3 modes (in Kelvin)?
3. Box contents: cable included? Power adapter? Manual in English or French?
4. Handle color: cream or wood? Is there a version with remote control?
5. Does the lamp get warm after several hours?
6. Packaging: can you ship in a plain box, with no price and no invoice inside? (Many orders are gifts.)
7. Can you add a printed gift message card when the customer asks for it?
8. Can you add my logo on the box later?

If everything is OK, I will order a sample now, then regular stock.
Thank you!
```

## Recherche d'un autre fournisseur (Alibaba)

## Message type pour tout nouveau fournisseur (dropshipping)

### Partie 1
```
Hello,

I am launching an online shop in France (Shopify) with a rechargeable bedside night lamp with a carry handle, a clock and touch control. I am looking for a long-term dropshipping partner and I am contacting several suppliers. I will choose the one with the most complete answers.

COMPLIANCE (required to sell in Europe)
1. Can you send the CE and RoHS certificates for this lamp?
2. Battery documents: UN38.3 test report and MSDS?
3. Manufacturer name and address, and an EU responsible person if you have one (GPSR)?
4. Energy label class and product information sheet?

DROPSHIPPING
5. Can you ship 1 unit directly to each of my customers in France?
6. Blind shipping: plain box, no price, no invoice, no supplier name inside?
7. Processing time before shipping, and tracking number for every order?
8. Can you connect to Shopify (or CJ / DSers), or do I send orders manually?

PRICE AND SHIPPING
9. Unit price for 1 pc (dropshipping), and for 30 / 100 pcs later?
10. Cheapest shipping to France for 1 parcel, and delivery time? Do you have an EU warehouse?
11. Are customs duties and VAT included (DDP / IOSS)?
```

### Partie 2
```
RETURNS AND DEFECTS
12. If a lamp arrives broken or defective, do you send a free replacement or refund?
13. What proof do you need (photo, video)? How many days do I have to report it?
14. For customer returns, do you have a return address in Europe?

PRODUCT DETAILS
15. Battery capacity (mAh), battery life at max and min brightness, charging time, charging port (USB-C?).
16. Color temperatures of the light modes (Kelvin), and dimming range.
17. Size, weight, box contents (cable? adapter? manual in English or French?).
18. Does the lamp get warm after several hours of use?
19. Can you send HD photos and videos without text that I can use?

SAMPLE AND BRANDING
20. Can I order 1 sample first? Total price with shipping?
21. Later: can you add my logo on the box, and a gift card in some orders?

Thank you! I can start quickly if everything is OK.
[Your name]
```
